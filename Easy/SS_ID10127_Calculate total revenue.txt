Calculate Samantha's and Lisa's total sales revenue : ID 10127


Question - What is the total sales revenue of Samantha and Lisa?

Table- sales_performance
id: bigint
sales_revenue: bigint
salesperson: text
widget_sales: bigint

#############################################################################
SOLUTION ->

sum(sales_revenue) as total_revenue
from sales_performance
where id in (3,7)
