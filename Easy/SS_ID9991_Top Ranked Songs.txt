Top Ranked Songs : ID 9991

Question -> Find songs that have ranked in the top position. Output the track name and the number of times it ranked at the top. Sort your records by the number of times the song was in the top position in descending order.

Table- >spotify_worldwide_daily_song_ranking
artist: text
id: bigint
position: bigint
region: text
stream_date: date
streams: bigint
trackname: text
url: text

########################################################################################

SOLUTION ->
select 
trackname,
count(trackname) as number_of_times_ranked_at_top
from spotify_worldwide_daily_song_ranking
where position = 1
group by trackname
order by number_of_times_ranked_at_top desc
;
