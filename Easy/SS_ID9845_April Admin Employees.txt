April Admin Employees: ID9845

Question - Find the number of employees working in the Admin department that joined in April or later, in any year.

Table - worker
department: text
first_name: text
joining_date: date
last_name: text
salary: bigint
worker_id: bigint


##########################################################################
Solution - 

select 
count(worker_id)
from worker
where department = 'Admin' and extract(month from joining_date) >= 4
;
